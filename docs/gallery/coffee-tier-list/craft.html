Flywheel, Saint Frank and Sightglass pour a great cup and still have a chair for you

Ten San Francisco shops, scored on the cup, the room, and your odds of sitting down. Pour-over counts double, because that’s why you came.

How to read a card

  • Cup. Pour-over quality: method, beans, care.
  • Room. Light, design, how long you’ll want to stay.
  • Seats. Your odds of a chair on a weekday morning.

Score = 2 × Cup + Room + Seats, out of 20. S is 17 and up; C is 11 and under.
