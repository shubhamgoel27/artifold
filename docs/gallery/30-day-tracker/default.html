How it works

  • Workout A and B alternate, 3 days a week.
  • Rest days: walk or stretch. They matter.
  • Warm up 5 minutes before every session.
  • Best started on a Monday.

Progression rules

  • Leave 1 to 2 reps "in the tank" on every set.
  • Missed reps or form broke down? Repeat that weight next time.
  • Felt easy with 3+ reps to spare? Go up one step early.

Rest between sets

  • 90 s on big lifts (squat, press, deadlift)
  • 60 s on rows and lunges
  • 45 s on core work
  • Stop if you feel sharp pain. Mild soreness is normal.

Your starting weights

Pick a weight you could lift about 15 times. Every target below updates from these numbers.

After day 30

  • Compare your Day 30 test reps with the 10 reps you did on Day 1. That gap is your new strength.
  • Keep going: repeat weeks 3 and 4, raising every starting weight by one step.
  • Once dumbbells get too light, move the squat and deadlift to a barbell.

General fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor first if you have an injury, heart condition, or are pregnant.
