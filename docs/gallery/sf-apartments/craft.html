Before you sign

Three questions per entry, each aimed at a weak mark.

1

989 20th St

  • The HOA’s pet rule, in writing.
  • Which garage space comes with the unit.
  • Unit orientation: courtyard or street.
2

400 Beale St

  • A signed dog clause from the owner and the board.
  • Which side the unit faces; the bridge side is louder.
  • Whether the pool and gym fees sit in the rent.
3

NEMA

  • The real rent after the move-in bonus, month by month.
  • Parking price today, not the old $450 quote.
  • A walk around 10th & Market after dark.

How the form was read

  1. No workplace was given, so commute is scored against two common anchors: Montgomery St in FiDi and the 4th & King Caltrain station. Times are map estimates, not trip-planner results.
  2. 989 20th St and 400 Beale are condo buildings. Each unit is rented by its owner, so rent, parking, and pet terms change from unit to unit. NEMA is one managed rental with one policy.
  3. Rents come from scattered listings, several undated. NEMA posts no prices, only “call for details.” Treat every dollar figure as a starting point to verify.
  4. Sun, vibe, and the 1–5 marks are judgment calls. The weight buttons exist so two people can argue with them.
