# v0.8 — fixes from three months of daily use

Driven by an audit of the live library (90 projects, 97 files, ~37 added
per month, 73 of them `/craft`-tagged), not by reading the code.

## The four problems

1. **Categorization is wrong ~19% of the time, unhelpful for another 19%.**
   `_categorize` did bare substring matching and returned the *first* dict
   key that hit. Real false positives: `'ai'` inside "airbnb" and "wait",
   `'rl'` inside "ctrl+alt+heal", `'ml '` in "ml engineer" beating Career's
   `'resume'` purely because Engineering is earlier in the dict. Format
   words (`tracker`, `guide`, `notes`, `card`, `review`, `story`) filed
   artifacts by their shape instead of their subject. 17/90 fell to "Other"
   even though 77/90 carry an LLM-written `intent` sentence the
   categorizer never read.

2. **`artifold designs --json` costs ~18k tokens per `/craft` run.**
   62 KB: every row, every palette. The skill only needs the last few
   values on each axis. Grows ~37 rows/month.

3. **Two provenance tags are written and thrown away.** `/craft` emits
   `artifold:conceit` (19 files) and `artifold:scale` (13 files);
   `detect.py` parses neither, so the skill's own "intensity rotates too"
   rule cannot work and the most interesting browse field never lands.

4. **v0.7.0 never shipped.** PyPI is on 0.6.2 (2026-06-08). Search,
   version diff, PDF export, provenance lifecycle and the whole `/craft`
   bundle are invisible to installers. No CI; `pytest` isn't even
   installed in `.venv`.

The four still in "Other" are honest: a World Cup sticker album, a routine
card with no topical word anywhere, and two green-card documents with no
recorded intent. Filing those would need vocabulary the defaults have no

### What the fix actually turned on

Three separate causes, and only the first was obvious:

1. Substring matching. `'ai'` in "wait" and "airbnb", `'rl'` in "ctrl",
   `'ml '` in "html ". Word boundaries alone fixed ~8 artifacts.
2. First-key-wins. `resume/resume.html` lost to Engineering's `'ml'`
   because Engineering was declared earlier in the dict. Scoring fixed it.
3. Field weighting, which was the surprise. Prose reaches for analogies
   its subject doesn't own — a mast-cell explainer written "with
   engineering analogies" scored Engineering over Health even after 1 and
   2. Trusting the path (3×) over recorded intent (1.5×) over body (1×)
   was what settled it, and it also rescued the generically-named files:
   `strength-training/tracker`, `sf-apartment-hunt/report`,
   `interview-prep/index` all file correctly off their directory now.

Two words had to be pulled after the first pass because they read as their
other meaning in this domain: `'cv'` (computer vision, not curriculum
vitae) and `'screen'` (a display, not a phone screen). `'explainer'` came
out of Education because it's the shape of half of all /craft output, not
a subject.

### Tradeoff accepted

Path weighting means a badly-named file with an accurate title now needs
roughly two body matches to outrank one path match. That is the right bias
for a library where /craft writes the filename from the topic slug, and
the wrong one for a folder of `untitled-1.html`. Worth revisiting if
anyone's library looks like the latter.
